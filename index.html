Privacy Policy & Disclaimers

All the financial data you enter is stored only on your device and is never transmitted anywhere. CanFIRE has no servers and no accounts. The only data that leaves your device is the subscription-related data described in this policy.
🧭

Who We Are

CanFIRE is published and operated by Tong Wee Seng (DS-Lab), Singapore, who is the data controller for the limited personal data described in this policy. For any privacy question or request, contact hello@canfire.app.

Because CanFIRE has no servers and no accounts, we hold almost no data about you. The only personal data processed on our behalf is the subscription information handled by RevenueCat, Apple, and Google, described below. Everything you enter into the app stays on your device and is never accessible to us.

🔒

Your Financial Data Stays on Your Device

On-Device Storage Only

Your expenses, income, assets, insurance details, and personal settings are stored locally on your device, in the app's private storage area, and are not uploaded to any server. That storage is protected by your device's own security — your passcode or biometric lock and the operating system's built-in device encryption. There is no cloud sync, no account creation, and no backend server. CanFIRE does not transmit your financial data to any third party.

Receipt Photos (Camera & Photo Library)

You can optionally attach a receipt image to a spending entry, using your camera or photo library. The app requests camera and photo permissions only for this purpose, and only when you initiate it. Receipt images are stored on your device alongside your other CanFIRE data and are never uploaded, analysed, or shared. If you never attach a receipt, these permissions are never used.

CPF Reference Figures

Once a week, CanFIRE requests publicly available CPF reference figures (BRS, FRS, ERS, and interest rates) from a small file hosted publicly on GitHub. No data you enter into the app is included in that request. As with any web request, GitHub receives your device's IP address in order to serve the file — we do not receive, see, or store it. If you are offline, the app falls back to figures bundled with the app.

Data Portability

To move your data to another device, use the Export / Restore feature in Settings. This creates a password-protected, AES-256 encrypted .canfire backup file that only you control. The file is never uploaded or shared automatically, and we cannot recover it or its password for you.

Your Control

You are in full control of your financial data. Edit or delete any entry directly within the app. To remove everything at once, use Settings → Clear All Data, or uninstall the app.

🚫

No Financial Tracking

CanFIRE does not collect usage analytics or financial telemetry. There is no advertising in the app, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. Your financial information is never uploaded to any server, including ours.

Standard device and transaction identifiers may be processed by Apple, Google, or our payment provider as part of subscription management, as described below. None of the financial data you enter — income, expenses, assets, or insurance — is shared with these parties.

🔗

Third-Party Services

RevenueCat (Subscription Management)

CanFIRE uses RevenueCat, a third-party subscription management SDK, to process Pro upgrades and restore purchases. To provide this service, RevenueCat may collect and process: a randomly generated device identifier, app version, device type and model, operating system and version, IP address (used by RevenueCat for fraud prevention and receipt validation), and purchase transaction data (product ID, transaction ID, purchase date, and subscription status).

RevenueCat does not receive your financial data or any personal information entered into the app — your income, expenses, assets, and insurance details never leave your device.

RevenueCat is headquartered in the United States, and data it processes is transferred to and stored there. CanFIRE takes reasonable steps to ensure this data is protected through RevenueCat's standard data processing agreements. For details on their practices, see the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Apple and Google

In-app purchases are processed by Apple (iOS) or Google (Android). Apple and Google act as independent controllers of your purchase and payment information under their own privacy policies — they do not process it on our behalf. For any subscription or payment data enquiry, contact Apple or Google directly through your device's app store.

🔔

Notifications

CanFIRE uses local notifications only — reminders are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device. No push token is created, stored, or transmitted to Apple, Google, or anyone else. You can disable reminders at any time in Settings or in your device's system settings.

🗑️

Data Retention & Deletion

Financial data you enter is stored locally on your device and retained until you delete it within the app or uninstall CanFIRE. We do not retain copies of this data. Reminder schedules are stored locally and removed when you disable notifications. Encrypted backup files you export are under your control — delete them from wherever you stored them.

Subscription and payment data is processed by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat, and retained in accordance with their respective policies. For subscription data enquiries or deletion requests, contact them directly, or email us and we will assist.

CPF & Government Data

Retirement sums (BRS / FRS / ERS) are taken from publicly published CPF Board figures and reviewed each January. CPF LIFE payout figures shown in the app are CanFIRE's own estimate, interpolated from CPF Board's published Standard Plan ranges — they are not CPF Board figures. All figures are for planning purposes only and may not reflect your actual CPF balances or entitlements. Please refer to cpf.gov.sg or your CPF statement for authoritative figures.

This app references publicly available CPF Board figures solely for planning purposes. It does not access, connect to, or retrieve data from your CPF account or any CPF Board systems.

Projections & Estimates

All financial projections are mathematical estimates based on your inputs and assumed growth rates. Past performance of any asset class does not guarantee future returns. Actual outcomes will differ from projections due to market conditions, inflation, policy changes, and personal circumstances.
