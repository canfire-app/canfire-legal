Privacy Policy & Legal Notices

🔒 Your Data Never Leaves Your Phone

Every number you enter — expenses, income, assets, insurance — is stored exclusively on your device using encrypted local storage.

CanFIRE has no servers, no accounts, and no internet connection to any backend. There is no way for CanFIRE (or anyone else) to access your financial data.

To move your data to another device, use the Export / Restore feature in Settings, which creates an AES-256 encrypted .canfire backup file that only you control.

👁️ No Tracking, No Analytics

CanFIRE does not collect usage analytics, crash reports, or any telemetry. All your financial data is stored exclusively on your device using encrypted local storage and is never uploaded to any server.

No personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, NRIC, income, or financial plan is ever transmitted from your device.

In-App Purchases (RevenueCat)

CanFIRE uses RevenueCat, a third-party subscription management SDK, to process Pro upgrades and restore purchases. RevenueCat receives a randomly generated device identifier and your subscription status to verify and manage your subscription. RevenueCat does not receive your name, NRIC, financial data, or any other personal information entered into the app.

RevenueCat Privacy Policy. In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google; see their respective privacy policies for payment data handling.

🛡️ Important Disclaimers

CanFIRE is a personal finance tracking tool, not a licensed financial adviser. Nothing in this app constitutes financial advice. Always consult a qualified professional before making investment or retirement decisions.

Not Financial Advice

CanFIRE is a personal retirement planning calculator. It is not a financial adviser, and nothing in this app constitutes financial advice, investment advice, or any form of regulated financial advisory service under the Financial Advisers Act (Cap. 110) or the Securities and Futures Act (Cap. 289) of Singapore.

All projections, estimates, and insights are based solely on the information you enter. They are for your own planning reference only. You should not make any financial decision based solely on outputs from this app.

For personalised financial, investment, or retirement advice, please consult a qualified financial professional.

CPF & Government Data

Retirement sums (BRS / FRS / ERS) and CPF LIFE payout estimates are sourced from cpf.gov.sg and updated periodically. Figures are for planning purposes only and may not reflect your actual CPF balances or entitlements. Please refer to cpf.gov.sg or your CPF statement for authoritative figures.

This app references publicly available CPF Board figures solely for planning purposes. It does not access, connect to, or retrieve data from your CPF account or any CPF Board systems.

Projections & Estimates

All financial projections are mathematical estimates based on your inputs and assumed growth rates. Past performance of any asset class does not guarantee future returns. Actual outcomes will differ from projections due to market conditions, inflation, policy changes, and personal circumstances.

No Affiliation

CanFIRE is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to CPF Board, any government body, financial institution, insurer, or investment product provider.
